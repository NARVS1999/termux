# ds-game-rom

Collection of 10 free Nintendo DS homebrew game ROMs (.nds). All games are original homebrew, freely distributed by their authors. Downloaded from GameBrew (dlhb.gamebrew.org) and official GitHub releases.

| # | Game | Author | Version | License | Source |
|---|------|--------|---------|---------|--------|
| 01 | T-Rex Runner Game DS | Fewnity | 2020 | Mixed | gamebrew.org/wiki/T-Rex_Runner_Game_DS |
| 02 | WolveSlayer | Firehazard Studio / AntonioND | v20240731 | MIT | gamebrew.org/wiki/WolveSlayer |
| 03 | Minicraft DS Edition | ArthurCose | 1.0.0-beta.3 | MIT | gamebrew.org/wiki/Minicraft_DS_Edition |
| 04 | Pingwinek Kelvin DS | Whatter | 2023 | Free | gamebrew.org/wiki/Pingwinek_Kelvin_DS |
| 05 | DScopter | vtor | 1.9 | Free | gamebrew.org/wiki/DSCopter |
| 06 | Jezzball DS | Erick Citaire | 1.3.0 | GPL | gamebrew.org/wiki/JezzballDS |
| 07 | MazeMan | Luke Rodgers | 2016 | GPL | gamebrew.org/wiki/MazeMan |
| 08 | DeS | BowersIndustry | 0.1.1 | Free | gamebrew.org/wiki/DeS |
| 09 | TerrariaDS | AzizBgBoss | 0.2 | MIT | github.com/AzizBgBoss/TerrariaDS |
| 10 | Marshmallow Duel DS | Sam Pospischil | 2 | GPL | gamebrew.org/wiki/Marshmallow_Duel_DS |

Notes:

- WolveSlayer needs the `wolveslayer/` data folder kept next to its .nds.
- Games 05-08 are original-era Slot-2 (PassMe) builds; 01-04 and 09 are standard Slot-1 builds.
- For emulators, melonDS/DeSmuME can load these; on real hardware a flashcart is required.
